# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Maintain respectful and constructive dialogue
- Receive feedback with grace and openness
- Prioritize community and project wellbeing
- Use inclusive and considerate language

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sharing private information without authorization
- Conduct that would be deemed inappropriate in professional contexts
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments

## Enforcement

Project maintainers handle reports of policy violations. Responses may range from
discussion to temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

## Scope

These guidelines apply across all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other communication channels associated with this project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening
an issue or contacting the project maintainers directly.
